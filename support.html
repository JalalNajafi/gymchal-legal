Support

Help for the GymChal iOS and Android app.

GymChal is a fitness accountability app: check in at your gym, keep a weekly target and streak, and optionally pair with a buddy for nudges and reactions.

Common questions

  • Delete my account: In the app, go to Me → Privacy → Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm. This erases your account and related server data. It cannot be undone.
  • Download a copy of my data: Me → Privacy → Consent & data rights → Download my data.
  • Change or withdraw consent: Me → Privacy → Consent & data rights. Withdrawing a required consent (account & pairing, or check-in location) will offer to erase your account.
  • Hide my gym name from my buddy: Me → Privacy → “Hide gym name from my buddy”.
  • Leave a pair: Me → Privacy → Leave pair, or the pair screens. Leaving is immediate. Your individual history is kept; the pair ends.
  • Notifications: You can allow or deny system permission, and you can turn optional nudge and reminder consent off in Consent & data rights.
  • TestFlight or App Store issues: Email us with your Apple ID email (you may hide it if you prefer) and a short description of the problem. Do not send one-time SMS codes.
